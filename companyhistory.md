Our Story

Our story began in Faisalabad in 1985, when our family entered Pakistan’s pulses trade. Through honest dealing, consistent quality and long-term relationships, we built more than three decades of practical experience in agricultural commodities.

Today, our family owns and operates two pulses-processing mills in Faisalabad. These facilities reflect our established presence in the pulses industry and give us hands-on experience in commodity procurement, quality assessment, processing, packaging and supply.

Building on this foundation, Sain Abdul Hakim and Company is expanding its reach to international markets by supplying Pakistani sesame seeds, pulses, rice and selected agricultural commodities.

Quality remains at the heart of everything we do. We ensure that every product is carefully checked against the buyer’s agreed specifications, with suitable packaging, proper documentation and professional handling from order confirmation through shipment.

Our goal is to represent Pakistan’s agricultural products responsibly and build lasting global partnerships based on transparency, dependable quality and consistent performance.
